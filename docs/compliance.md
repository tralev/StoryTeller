**App A (Mobile) — Llama 3.2 3B bundling:** The Game Master model (Llama 3.2 3B) is downloaded on-device during first launch, not bundled in the app binary. This avoids app store binary size limits and keeps the model under the end-user download terms.

**Attribution requirements:** Llama 3.2 requires displaying "Built with Llama" attribution. This should appear in the app's About screen.

## App Store Content Policies

### Apple App Store

- **Age Rating:** The app generates dark fantasy content with violence and mature themes. Minimum age rating: **17+**.
- **AI Content Disclosure:** Apple requires disclosure that the app generates AI content. Add an app description note: "This app displays AI-generated text, images, and music."
- **User-Generated Content:** If Game Master chat is considered UGC, a content reporting mechanism may be required. Since responses are generated on-device with no server, this is a lighter requirement.
- **Binary Size:** Since the model is downloaded on first launch (not bundled), the app binary stays under 200 MB. The download prompt must be clear to the user.

### Google Play Store

- **Age Rating:** Dark fantasy content → **Mature 17+**.
- **AI Content:** Google requires generative AI content disclosure. Use the "AI-generated content" declaration in the Play Console.
- **Model Download:** The ~2 GB model download on first launch must happen over Wi-Fi (use `ConnectivityManager` to check). The Play Store allows expansion files (OBB) up to 2 GB, but on-device download avoids this complexity.

## Content Disclaimer

The Forge generates all story content automatically. Recommended disclaimer for both apps:

> "All story content, illustrations, and music in this application are generated by artificial intelligence. Characters, events, and worlds are fictional. Any resemblance to real persons or events is coincidental. The content reflects the creative output of AI models and is not manually curated."

---

## Privacy

- **App B (The Forge):** No network calls except initial model download from Hugging Face. No telemetry, no analytics, no data collection.
- **App A (The Player):** No network calls except first-launch model download. All Game Master processing is on-device. Reader questions and GM responses never leave the device. Cloud sync (if enabled) only syncs `save/` — small JSON files with reading progress, not content.

No privacy policy complexity. Both apps are effectively offline utilities.
